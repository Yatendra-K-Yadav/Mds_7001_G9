Project Title: Analyzing Commuting Patterns and Transit Demand in South East Queensland: A Study on Translink Origin-Destination Trips (2022–Present)
1. Background & Motivation
Public transportation is critical for urban mobility, economic productivity, and sustainable development across South East Queensland (SEQ). In the post-pandemic era, commuting behaviors and transit demand have shifted significantly due to hybrid work arrangements and population growth across Brisbane and neighboring regions. Understanding origin-destination (OD) passenger dynamics is essential for network optimization, congestion relief, and proactive resource allocation.
2. Dataset Overview
This project utilizes the Translink Public Transport Origin-Destination Trips (2022 Onwards) dataset published by the Queensland Government Open Data Portal. The dataset provides aggregated monthly passenger journey records across Translink's multi-modal transit network (including bus, rail, ferry, and light rail), capturing spatial dimensions (Origin and Destination stops/stations), route directions, temporal attributes, and patronage volumes.

3. Research Questions / Objectives
This study aims to address the following core questions:

Spatial Flow & Network Bottlenecks: What are the high-density commuter corridors and busiest OD pairs across SEQ?

Temporal Dynamics: How do travel volumes fluctuate across peak vs. off-peak hours, weekdays vs. weekends, and seasonal trends from 2022 onwards?

Predictive Insights: Can machine learning or time-series models accurately forecast passenger load and identify underserviced routes to inform transit scheduling?
